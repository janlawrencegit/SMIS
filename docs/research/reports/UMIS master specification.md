# Build UMIS foundation-first, ledger-true, regulator-ready

UMIS should be built the way every mature student information system is built. Start with a shared person and security layer. Next comes an effective-dated academic foundation (organization, levels, configurable term types, calendars). Curriculum and catalog follow, then term-specific delivery (sections, enrollment, grades). Finance, aid, services and portals sit on top. Every leading product (PeopleSoft Campus Solutions, Workday Student, Ellucian Banner) draws its module boundaries this way, and vendors say outright that the academic structure must exist before any transaction is entered ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G11181_01/cs92pbr35/eng/cs/lsfn/UnderstandingAcademicStructure-ab5851.html); [Workday](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/student-for-administrators/organizations-and-academic-foundation.html)). For a Philippine university, three additions turn that generic design into the UMIS master specification. First, a rules layer that is configurable, not hard-coded: 1.0–5.0 grading with INC/4.0 lapse rules, unit-load and scholastic-standing policies, and semester/trimester/midyear term types, all seeded with Philippine defaults. Second, UMIS's own append-only double-entry student ledger, built for per-unit tuition, installments due by exam period, promissory notes, government-sponsor receivables, and BIR invoicing or e-invoicing (or COA receipts for SUCs). The FinDesk `Ledger::post` is not reused. Third, a compliance layer covering CHED HEMIS exports, RA 10931 Free Higher Education and TES reporting, Special Orders, CAV, the Data Privacy Act and NPC circulars, and the RA 11984 "No Permit, No Exam" override. Implementation failures elsewhere argue for a phased Phase 1. It should cover the platform, the foundation, curriculum, admissions, student records, scheduling, enrollment, grading and transcripts, assessment and cashiering, holds and clearance, core portals, and the statutory reports. Degree-audit what-if, full aid billing, LMS standards, HR/payroll and verifiable credentials come in later phases. Several regulatory details remain open: the BIR e-invoicing deadline of 31 Dec 2026 and whether it covers schools, refund percentages, TES amounts, and the HEMIS template columns. They are listed in a separate verification register at the end and must not be hard-coded.

## How to read this specification: four confidence tags

The specification is synthesized from five research notes compiled on 21 Nov 2026. Each claim carries one of four tags, so planners can tell evidence from design. **[V]** means verified in the research session from the cited primary or reputable secondary source; any claim with an inline URL and no tag is [V]. **[S]** means a statute, standard or reference cited from established background knowledge and not re-fetched in the session, marked "[S]" or "[ref, not fetched]" in the notes. Treat [S] items as high-likelihood but re-check section numbers before quoting them as law. **[L]** means low-authority or conflicting sources, such as Scribd copies of handbooks, legal-commentary blogs and commercial guides. Never encode an [L] value without confirmation. **[I]** means a design inference: a proposed UMIS requirement drawn from the cited material and not stated by any source. Requirement tables use these tags in a "Basis" column. Everything tagged [S], [L] or "verify" is collected again in the verification register.

Two project constraints override anything in the notes. UMIS is a standalone Laravel 13 / PostgreSQL 16 / Inertia + Vue 3 + TypeScript / Tailwind 4 / Redis codebase tested with Pest, Vitest and Playwright. One note suggests reusing FinDesk's `Ledger::post` and its Bootstrap toast notifications; neither applies, and UMIS defines its own ledger and UI notification layer. Grading scales, term types, load limits, honors thresholds, refund bands and fee rules are **institution-configurable data**. Philippine conventions are seed defaults only.

## Every leading system layers the same nine domains

Mature products separate a shared person/community layer, an academic foundation, curriculum and catalog (kept apart from scheduled sections), admissions, student records, advising and degree audit, student financials, financial aid, and HR/finance. PeopleSoft calls its base layer Campus Community. It holds service indicators (holds) and the "3Cs": communications, checklists and comments ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E56917_01/cs9pbr4/eng/cs/lscc/index.html)). Its term and session dates are shared so that "Student Financials, Financial Aid, and Student Records … all must use dates that match", because terms "affect class enrollment, financial aid, billing, tuition refunds, and statistical reporting" ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E17221_01/cs9pbr2/eng/psbooks/lsfn/htm/lsfn07.htm)). Workday organizes Student into Records and Advising, Financials, Recruiting and Admissions, and an "Academic Foundation" that each functional track reviews first ([Workday](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/student-financials-for-administrators/academic-foundation-review.html)). Banner encodes the same split in its object names (S = Student, G = General) and keys all records to an internal person identifier (PIDM), with the student record effective-dated by term ([UNM naming conventions](https://artsci-da.unm.edu/sites/default/files/Banner%20Naming%20Conventions.pdf); [Missouri State ODS](https://prodssb.missouristate.edu/metadata/AS_GENERAL_STUDENT_COMP_target.html)).

The details that matter for UMIS are structural. In PeopleSoft, "academic programs are part of academic careers", plans and subplans subdivide programs, and "courses and classes are … directly linked to terms and sessions" ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G11181_01/cs92pbr35/eng/cs/lsfn/UnderstandingAcademicStructure-ab5851.html)). Different careers can have different term structures. A session subdivides a term, and Oracle warns that without session time periods "the system denies access to enrollment functions" ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E17221_01/cs9pbr2/eng/psbooks/lsfn/htm/lsfn07.htm)). Workday's calendar chain runs period types → calendar template → dated academic year → periods. Its calendar policies are effective-dated and assigned to units or programs, and an institution designation "cannot be removed once objects are associated" ([Workday](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/student-for-administrators/organizations-and-academic-foundation.html)). Banner stores **credit hours and billing hours as separate fields** on each registration and keeps registration history in a separate audit table ([Toledo ODS](https://reports.utoledo.edu/metadata/metadata85/STUDENT_COURSE_ODS_target.html); [Toledo audit ODS](https://wrl.utoledo.edu/metadata/metadata92/STUDENT_COURSE_REG_AUDIT_ODS_target.html)). That split lets Philippine lab, NSTP and tutorial units bill differently from academic units [I].

Market structure is now a risk factor in its own right. Anthology filed for Chapter 11 on 29 Sep 2025, and Ellucian closed its acquisition of Anthology's SIS/ERP business around the turn of 2026, adding more than 260 customers ([Campus Technology](https://campustechnology.com/articles/2026/01/05/ellucian-officially-acquires-anthologys-sis-and-erp-business.aspx); [Ellucian](https://www.ellucian.com/newsroom/ellucian-completes-acquisition-anthologys-sis-and-erp-business)). Ellucian now owns Banner, Colleague and Anthology Student. At the open-source end, OpenEduCat 19.0 (LGPL-3.0) ships core, admission, attendance, classroom, exam, facility, fees, library, parent and timetable modules ([GitHub](https://github.com/openeducat/openeducat_erp)). Fedena's core covers admission, student information, attendance, timetable, examination, finance and HR, with paid add-ons ([LinuxLinks](https://www.linuxlinks.com/fedena/)). Both are K-12-style suites. Neither has a multi-career, effective-dated academic structure or a rules-based degree audit [I], so they are pattern sources, not templates. The patterns worth borrowing are concrete. ERPNext Education moved from a bespoke Fees doctype to standard Sales Invoices (v15.1), so fees are ordinary receivables in a general ledger ([Frappe](https://docs.frappe.io/education/fees.md)). OpenEduCat keeps the applicant (`op.admission`) separate from the student and creates the student only at enrollment ([OpenEduCat](https://newdocs.openeducat.org/admission/)). Koha resolves circulation rules on a patron-category × item-type × branch matrix, most specific first, with a mandatory default ([Koha manual](https://koha-community.org/manual/22.05/en/html/circulation.html)). One privacy caution: the OpenEduCat 18.0 admission module sends instance telemetry to the vendor ([Odoo apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/openeducat_admission)).

### Failures cluster in budgets, customization, interfaces and untested peaks

University ERP projects fail in repeatable ways, and each case translates into a UMIS design or governance rule.

| Case | What happened | UMIS rule it implies [I] |
|---|---|---|
| Minnesota State (Workday) | Budget rose from $151.1M to $290.4M. Student go-live slipped from fall 2026 to fall 2029. Faculty pay was computed across Workday plus two legacy systems, and auditors found inaccurate pay ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/off-prem/2026/04/08/minnesota-payroll-problems-grew-after-workday-say-auditors/5221270)) | Keep no critical calculation outside the system. Every interface gets an owner, a reconciliation report and an end date |
| WashU (Workday) | About $265M over seven or more years (about $16,000 per student), disclosed only after protests. Replaced about 80 systems ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/12/washington_university_workday_costs_revealed/)) | Publish an honest TCO with contingency early |
| SUNY Erie (Workday) | Called a "failure" after $12M in five years. $5.2M approved to move to Banner ([GovTech](https://www.govtech.com/education/higher-ed/erie-community-college-to-spend-5-2m-replacing-disastrous-erp-system)) | Own the data model and keep exportable data to reduce lock-in |
| Edinburgh (Oracle Cloud) | £25M contract, £37M final. Late stipends, more than £300k misallocated. Processes flagged "red" went ahead anyway. Built for finance-trained users ([Panorama](https://www.panorama-consulting.com/edinburgh-universitys-failed-implementation/)) | Fit-to-standard governance gate. Design for non-specialist faculty and department users |
| UMass Amherst (PeopleSoft) | Crashed the day before classes, down 4 days. No load testing; password resets overloaded it ([CIO.com](https://www.cio.com/article/272120/enterprise-resource-planning-university-erp-big-mess-on-campus.html)) | Load-test enrollment and term-start surges, including password resets |
| Stanford, Northeastern, Indiana, Cleveland State | Departmental customization, 22 modules bought at once, about 3,000 students wrongly denied aid, software alleged "virtually unusable" ([CIO.com](https://www.cio.com/article/272120/enterprise-resource-planning-university-erp-big-mess-on-campus.html)) | Phase the scope. Run aid and payroll in parallel with legacy before cutover. Run a customization review board |

The cited survey figure that university ERP projects cost about 5× projection comes without sample details. The "50% overrun" figure comes from a secondary review [L] ([Bettera](https://www.bettera.co/post/higher-education-erp-failure-research)). Read the direction of both, not the magnitude. No Philippine SIS post-mortem was found.

## An eighteen-step dependency chain fixes the build order

No vendor publishes an end-to-end implementation sequence, but the documented dependencies fix one. PeopleSoft requires each term to have a session, session time periods before enrollment, and holiday schedules from HR ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E17221_01/cs9pbr2/eng/psbooks/lsfn/htm/lsfn07.htm)). Workday requires calendars before they are assigned to units or programs, and program-of-study types before programs ([Workday](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/student-for-administrators/organizations-and-academic-foundation.html)). Assessment depends on enrollment and fee schedules. Clearance aggregates holds from every office. Faculty load needs HR faculty records. Revenue posting needs the GL. WashU ran a 16-month discovery phase before building and treated foundations as a separate workstream ([WashU Sunrise](https://sunrise.wustl.edu/?p=450)).

| 3 | Careers/levels (UG, graduate, law/medicine if any, SHS track if any) | 2 | Phase 1 |

The Phase 1 boundary is a recommendation, not a finding. Northeastern's 22-module big bang and Minnesota State's split payroll both argue for delivering the foundation and the student-records spine first. Every Phase 1 module also has to work end to end for a full term cycle: admit, enroll, assess, collect, grade, report. A partial cutover would leave a critical calculation in legacy, which is the Minnesota failure mode [I].

## Module-by-module functional requirements

Each module below opens with its purpose and the evidence behind it, followed by a requirements table. IDs are stable handles for Phase 1 backlog planning.

### Platform services: identity, workflow, holds and the 3Cs underpin every module

The platform layer copies the vendor pattern of configurable holds, append-only histories and a shared communications/checklist layer. PeopleSoft service indicators have an effect (positive or negative), one or more service impacts (registration, transcript and so on), term- or date-based validity, a reason, an amount, the placing office, release security, and an audit history that keeps every assignment and release ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E17221_01/cs9pbr2/eng/psbooks/lscc/htm/lscc39.htm)). Students see only negative indicators, labeled "Holds" ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/applications/peoplesoft/campus-solutions/9.2.038/campus-self-service/using-self-service-service-indicators-data.html)). Institutional practice is that only the placing office releases a hold ([FSCJ](https://training.fscj.edu/images/pdfs/cs/intro_serv_indic_checklists.pdf)). Standard workflow is a draft → submitted → reviewed → approved/rejected state machine with role-routed steps; OpenEduCat registers and ERPNext invoices are draft-first ([OpenEduCat](https://newdocs.openeducat.org/admission/create-register/); [Frappe](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/fee-schedule)). Workday routes workflow steps to security groups, not to individuals ([Workday](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/security-for-administrators/security-policy-configuration-and-activation.html)).

| ID | Requirement / business rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|

| PLT-02 | Name, address and contact history kept effective-dated, never overwritten | [I] |

| PLT-04 | Generic workflow engine: versioned definitions, sequential or parallel steps routed to role + scope rules (e.g. "dean of the student's college"), SLA and escalation, actions approve/reject/return/delegate with comment. Initiator ≠ approver is enforced where configured | Workday; OpenEduCat [V]; [I] |
| PLT-05 | Checklists (templates → assigned items with due dates and status), communication templates with logged sends (email/SMS/in-app), categorized comments attachable to any person or record | PeopleSoft 3Cs [V] |

### Academic foundation and calendar: term types are data, not code

The foundation must handle semester, trimester, quarter and midyear/summer institutions without code changes. UP Diliman's academic year is two semesters of at least 16 weeks / 100 class days plus a six-week summer whose class work is "equivalent to class work in one semester". A student who skipped only summer is not "rejoining" ([UPD Glossary](https://our.upd.edu.ph/files/catalogue/GLOSSARY.pdf)). DLSU runs three trimesters of about 14 weeks ([DLSU](https://www.dlsu.edu.ph/?p=14587)), and Mapúa is also on trimesters ([Mapúa](https://support.mapua.edu.ph/kb/article/53/grading-system)). The opening month has changed over time. CHED's 2019 push for an August opening for SUCs/LUCs was later clarified as a recommendation needing BOR approval ([GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/news/nation/691227/shifting-start-of-academic-year-to-august-only-a-recommendation-says-ched/story)). In June 2025 CHED RO XI consulted HEIs on adjusting AY 2025-2026 again; the final memo was not found ([CHED RO XI](https://ro11.ched.gov.ph/2025/06/23/ched-ro-xi-holds-online-consultation-with-davao-region-heis-on-the-adjustment-of-ay-2025-2026/10863/news-and-activities/technical-assistance-to-heis/)). PeopleSoft's term table carries dates, academic year, weeks of instruction, a refund point and sequential term numbers. Oracle advises numbering terms in increments of two or three and keeping large gaps in class numbers between terms ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E17221_01/cs9pbr2/eng/psbooks/lsfn/htm/lsfn07.htm)).

### Curriculum, programs and catalog: version every requirement against the catalog year

The core distinction is between the static offering (program, curriculum version, catalog course) and the term-specific delivery. Each student is audited against the curriculum version in force when they entered. PeopleSoft matches career, program, plan, subplan and precondition against requirement groups using the student's **requirement term (catalog year)** ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/applications/peoplesoft/campus-solutions/9.2.038/academic-advisement/understanding-academic-requirement-groups.html)). Its requirements are built bottom-up: course lists → line items → requirements → groups ([Oracle AA PDF](https://docs.oracle.com/en/applications/peoplesoft/campus-solutions/9.2.038/academic-advisement/academic-advisement.pdf)). Degree Works supports wildcards such as "any 500-level SPN" ([Missouri State](https://www.missouristate.edu/Registrar/_Files/DWUsersGuide516AULExceptions.pdf)). In UP vocabulary, a "Program" is degree + discipline and a "Subject" is a course. A corequisite is "taken simultaneously within a semester" ([UPD Glossary](https://our.upd.edu.ph/files/catalogue/GLOSSARY.pdf)). CHED discipline PSGs prescribe minimum curricula, faculty qualifications and practicum hours, so curricula must be versioned against the PSG CMO number [S] ([CHED issuances](https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/issuances/)). CHED CMO 1 s.2025 adds micro-credentials ([CHED 2025 CMOs](https://legacy.ched.gov.ph/2025-ched-memorandum-orders/)). Program vs plan needs a decision. PeopleSoft and Workday separate the program (what a student is admitted to and graduates from) from the plan (major). A CHED program such as "BS Accountancy" usually maps 1:1, while "BSEd major in Math" needs a plan/major level [I].

### Admissions: the applicant stays separate from the student until matriculation

Admissions runs from inquiry to matriculation. An admission cycle (OpenEduCat's "register") controls quotas, fees and open/close dates, and accepts no applications while in draft ([OpenEduCat](https://doc.openeducat.org/19.0/applications/enrollment/enrollment-admission-register.html)). The application fee defaults from the register and can be overridden, with a due date that confirms the application ([OpenEduCat](https://doc.openeducat.org/19.0/applications/enrollment/enrollment-registration.html)). Sources disagree on exact application state names ([OpenEduCat](https://newdocs.openeducat.org/admission/admission-process/)), so UMIS defines its own states. RA 10931 requires SUCs/LUCs to report entrance-exam takers and results to CHED within five working days after registration closes [V] ([IRR](https://unifast.gov.ph/assets/pdf/guidelines/IRR10931.pdf)). Admissions data therefore feeds a statutory report.

| ID | Requirement / business rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|

### Student records: the program stack is effective-dated and history is never rewritten

Students change status in ways the model must keep. PeopleSoft gives each student a "program/plan stack" by career and requires a valid plan for term activation ([Pitt guide](https://registrar.pitt.edu/sites/default/files/pdf/PS%20Student%20Program_Plan%20Guide.pdf)). ### Class scheduling: manual building with live hard-constraint checks, solver optional

Registrars in practice copy the prior term forward and edit, with real-time conflict checks. Automated timetabling is formalized as curriculum-based course timetabling (ITC-2007 Track 3). Registration conflict checks must include lecture, lab and recitation, and alternate-week patterns can produce false conflicts ([Ole Miss](https://olemiss.edu/encompass/students/common-registration-errors/)).

### Enrollment: every check returns a typed error, every override grants exactly one

### Grading and transcripts: scheme, basis and grade value with independent flags

Grading must be configurable without code. PeopleSoft's model is the reference: a grading scheme has grade bases (graded, pass/no-pass, audit). Each grade value carries grade points and independent flags: include in GPA, earn credit, in-progress, exclude progress units, valid attempt, convert-to grade, faculty-selectable. Penalty grades are defined per basis ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G40826_01/cs92pbr36/eng/cs/lssr/SettingUpYourSystemforGrading-ab47e2.html)). Workday likewise flags "Include in GPA" per grade ([Workday](https://doc.workday.com/admin-guide/en-us/student/student-records/academic-records/academic-policies/concept--grade-point-averages.html)).

The Philippine defaults are documented, and they vary by school. On Mapúa's scale, 1.00 is Excellent, 3.00 the lowest passing grade and 5.00 Failure, with percentage cut-offs set per course syllabus. "I" must be completed within the next trimester. "W" must be filed by week 7, at most twice per course, and never for PE/NSTP. Absences of 20% or more yield 5.00, and Audit is excluded from the TOR ([Mapúa](https://support.mapua.edu.ph/kb/article/53/grading-system)). UP adds **4.0 "Conditional"**, removable only by re-exam (pass → 3, fail → 5, not removed → 5). INC is given when class standing is passing but the final exam or other requirements were missed. Scholastic standing is set by the percentage of units below 3: warning at 25–49%, probation at 50–75%, dismissal from the college at 76% or more, permanent disqualification at 100% ([UPD Glossary](https://our.upd.edu.ph/files/catalogue/GLOSSARY.pdf)). UP's removal window is one academic year, and a 4.0 earned after completing an INC must be removed within the original INC window ([UPD OUR](https://our.upd.edu.ph/files/acadinfo/REMOVAL%20OF%20INCOMPLETE.pdf)). The source PDFs were only partly read, and a 2021 OVPAA memo may have changed the deadlines. No CHED-wide grading scale was found; HEIs set their own [V/L] ([Respicio](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/legal-requirements-for-granting-latin-honors-in-philippine-schools)). UP distinguishes several averages: GWA (all subjects), Curriculum Weighted Average (used for shifting), Cumulative WA for graduate programs, and an honors average limited to resident credit ([UPD](https://www.our.upd.edu.ph/files/acadinfo/GRADUATION%20WITH%20HONORS.pdf)).

### Degree audit, graduation and honors: exceptions are first-class and break visibly

Degree Works and PeopleSoft both organize requirements in blocks or groups keyed to catalog year. In Degree Works, unusable courses fall to "Not Counted" or "Audited, Dropped, Failed, Repeated" blocks. Student-specific deviations are exceptions: Also Allow, Apply Here, Force Complete (which "should be among the least used"), Substitute, and Remove Course/Change Limit. Each exception "ties to a specific block, which is like an address", and a program change breaks it. Exceptions cannot be edited, only deleted and re-entered ([Missouri State](https://www.missouristate.edu/Registrar/_Files/DWUsersGuide516AULExceptions.pdf)). What-If runs the same engine against a hypothetical program or catalog year "without officially committing the changes" ([Missouri State](https://www.missouristate.edu/Registrar/_Files/DWUsersGuide516Registrar.pdf)).

Philippine graduation adds honors rules, Board/Academic Council conferment and Special Orders. UP's Latin-honors minimums are 1.20 / 1.45 / 1.75. Honors require 75% of units earned in residence plus two years in residence immediately before graduation, and only resident credit counts toward the honors average. Candidates must carry at least 15 units (or the normal load) each semester, with sworn health, course-unavailability or employment exceptions filed during the underloaded semester. Cheating, or suspension of a year or more, disqualifies a candidate. Shiftees' electives are chosen in a fixed priority order ([UPD Graduation with Honors](https://www.our.upd.edu.ph/files/acadinfo/GRADUATION%20WITH%20HONORS.pdf)). CHED sets no uniform national honors cutoff, and secondary sources conflict [L] ([Respicio](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/legal-requirements-for-granting-latin-honors-in-philippine-schools); [lawyer-philippines.com](https://www.lawyer-philippines.com/articles/ched-guidelines-on-awarding-latin-honors-in-philippine-universities)). A Certificate of Completion is issued while BOR conferment is pending ([UPD Glossary](https://our.upd.edu.ph/files/catalogue/GLOSSARY.pdf)). Approved Special Orders are returned to the HEI for safekeeping ([CHED RO XI RM 030 s.2022](https://ro11.ched.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/RM-NO.-030-S.-2022.pdf)).

### Student financials: a UMIS-owned double-entry ledger with immutable postings

Fees are a receivable subledger of a double-entry GL. ERPNext posts each fee as a Sales Invoice with receivable account, income account and cost center, and receives payment through Payment Entries ([Frappe](https://docs.frappe.io/education/fees.md)). Banner stores AR detail in one table where the detail-code type determines charge vs payment ([Missouri State ODS](https://prodssb.missouristate.edu/metadata/Accounts_Receivable_ODS_index.html)). Banner also has an "Account Detail Purge" process ([Jefferson](https://banadmin.jefferson.edu/Banner_Docs/Banner_Accounts_Receivable_User_Guide.pdf)). UMIS deliberately goes further and never deletes posted entries. UMIS defines its **own ledger**. The FinDesk `Ledger::post` mentioned in one note is a different project and is not reused. UMIS's design is `journal_entries` + `journal_lines` with Σdebit = Σcredit enforced by a deferred constraint trigger, plus a `student_ledger_entries` subledger view. Postings are append-only, and corrections are reversals [I].

Philippine assessment is per unit plus lab fees per lab subject plus miscellaneous and one-time fees, paid as a downpayment and then installments tied to exam periods. UE charges a processing fee for cancellation before classes and 5% of the balance after the last installment ([UE](https://www.ue.edu.ph/mla/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/COLLEGES-MANILA-SY-2025-2026-COLLEGE-OF-ARTS-SCIENCES.pdf)). DLSU-D requires 70% of the semester's fees before finals ([DLSU-D](https://www.dlsud.edu.ph/announcement/shs/2026/SHS-SCHEDULE-OF-PAYMENTS-2ND-SEM-SY25-26.pdf)). **RA 11984 (No Permit, No Exam Prohibition Act, 2024)** lets students with unpaid fees sit exams if they present an SWDO/DSWD certificate of disadvantaged status. Schools may still require promissory notes and withhold records ([Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/report/philippines-no-permit-no-exam-prohibition-act/)); the IRR was not retrieved. Withdrawal refund percentages under CHED MORPHE conflict across sources [L]: 80/50/20% by week, 90/80%, a 25% retention, and a background-knowledge version of 10%/20%/full charges ([Respicio](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/are-withdrawal-fees-legal-philippine-rules-on-school-tuition-refunds-after-enrollment)). The refund engine must therefore be policy-driven.

Tax rules split by institution type. Under the EOPT Act (RA 11976) and RR 7-2024, the **invoice replaced the official receipt** as the primary document for services, and remaining ORs could be used once stamped "Invoice" ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2024/eopt-invoicing-clarified.html)). RR 11-2025 (CREATE MORE) mandates e-invoicing and sales reporting to the EIS for large taxpayers, e-commerce sellers and **CAS/CBA users** ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2025/paperles-invoicing-and-sales-reporting.html)). RR 26-2025 moved the deadline to 31 Dec 2026 ([PwC Tax Alert 29](https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/tax-alerts/2025/tax-alert-29.html)). RMC 98-2026 requires a PTI for e-invoicing, then EIS certification within six months. CAS-printed invoices are not e-invoices, corrections are made by credit note, never by editing, and as of 14 Sep 2026 no e-invoicing service provider was BIR-accredited ([KPMG PH](https://kpmg.com/ph/en/insights/2026/09/special-intax-september-2026-issue-1-volume-2.html)). Whether VAT-exempt educational institutions or SUCs fall under the mandate was **not found**. SUCs use COA Official Receipts (AF 51) [S].

### Scholarships and StuFAP: stackable sponsors with their own receivables and billing

## Roles are bundles; scope, separation of duties and audit do the real work

Banner, PeopleSoft and Workday all use three layers. Functional permissions are granted through named groups: Banner security classes, PeopleSoft permission lists → roles, Workday domain policies. A data-scope layer restricts rows by institution, campus, career, program or department: Banner FGAC on Oracle VPD, PeopleSoft secure views, Workday role-based groups on organizations. Business-process security governs who initiates and who approves ([Drake](https://drake.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/2025/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=146902); [FHDA VBS](https://fhdafiles.fhda.edu/downloads/eisDocs/ValueBasedSecurityOverviewPP.pdf); [Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/applications/peoplesoft/campus-solutions/9.2.038/campus-solutions-application-fundamentals/securing-academic-structure.html); [Workday](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/security-for-administrators/security-policy-configuration-and-activation.html)). Two vendor lessons carry over. PeopleSoft allows exactly one row-security role per user, and auditing scope requires hand-written SQL, so inherited data scopes are hard to audit ([Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G11181_01/cs92pbr35/eng/cs/lsfn/ManagingDepartmentSecurity.html); [MOSC](https://community.oracle.com/mosc/discussion/4558683/sql-to-return-all-row-level-security-assigned-to-an-oprid)). Workday holds security changes as pending until they are activated ([Workday](https://doc.workday.com/workday-education/en-us/course-manuals/security-for-administrators/security-policy-configuration-and-activation.html)). Spatie's laravel-permission "teams" feature scopes assignments to a single `team_id` dimension ([Spatie](https://spatie.be/docs/laravel-permission/v6/basic-usage/teams-permissions)), and practitioners report limits when it is used for hierarchy ([GitHub #2657](https://www.github.com/spatie/laravel-permission/discussions/2657)). UMIS should therefore use Spatie (or equivalent) for permission bundles plus a custom `role_assignments(user, role, scope_type, scope_id, valid_from, valid_to, granted_by, reason)` table. Scope inherits downward through the org closure table, Laravel Policies evaluate attributes, and PostgreSQL row-level security on grades, health, discipline and student accounts acts as a backstop. That requires a non-owner DB role, `SET LOCAL` of the user and scopes per transaction, and awareness of PgBouncer transaction mode [I].

Separation of duties follows Workday's model of initiator ≠ approver checks on workflow steps, supported by data. For grades, faculty encode, the chair or dean approves, the registrar finalizes, and post-finalization changes need a different approver plus before/after logging. For finance, cashiers post, separate approvers handle refunds, voids and write-offs, and static conflicts such as Cashier plus Refund Approver are blocked at assignment time. For security, nobody grants their own roles and high-privilege grants need a second approver. Delegations are time-boxed assignments that cannot carry SoD-conflicting permissions. Break-glass access requires a reason, alerts the DPO and expires within hours. Impersonation is read-only by default, shows a UI banner, logs both actor and impersonated IDs, and never targets a higher-privileged user. Columns are classified Public / Internal / Confidential / SPI. Serializers drop SPI fields unless the actor holds `*.view_sensitive`, and **every read of sensitive records is logged**, not just writes. An access-review report (who holds what, in which scope, granted by whom, expiring when) supports NPC accountability [I].

Audit and security baselines combine OWASP and NPC requirements. OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 was released 30 May 2025 with three levels and about 350 requirements ([OWASP ASVS](https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS)). UMIS should target Level 2 overall and Level 3 controls for authentication, authorization, audit and cryptography in the grades, finance and SPI modules [I]. **NPC Circular 2023-06** took effect 30 March 2024 (compliance by 30 March 2025). It covers private and public sectors, repealed government-only Circular 16-01, and requires a privacy impact assessment per processing system, access only from authorized software and devices, and storage only as long as necessary ([NPC](https://privacy.gov.ph/?p=46138); [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/data-technology/philippines-minimum-requirements-for-security-of-personal-data-issued-by-the-national-privacy-commission)). Its full text could not be fetched, so specific control wording (MFA, algorithms, log retention) must be checked. **NPC Circular 2022-04** requires DPO and system registration through NPCRS for organizations with 250 or more employees or sensitive data on 1,000 or more individuals ([Circular 2022-04](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Circular-2022-04.pdf)), which any university will meet. The Education Sector council stresses that schools "are accountable for all personal data it collects and processes, even if they properly obtained the consent" ([Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1336881/respect-kids-privacy-amid-online-learning-council-urges)).

The UMIS API follows widely documented practice, cited here from references not re-fetched in the session [ref, not fetched]. Resources are plural nouns nested one level, with stable ULIDs and the student number as a lookup key. Workflow transitions are explicit actions (`POST /grade-submissions/{id}/approve`) so policies and SoD attach cleanly. The API is versioned in the URI (`/api/v1`) with Deprecation/Sunset headers. Pagination is by cursor with `updated_since` for delta sync. Errors follow RFC 9457 problem+json. `Idempotency-Key` is required on payments, refunds and enrollments (stored with a unique constraint and a 24–72 h TTL). ETag/If-Match guards grade sheets and profiles. A transactional outbox emits domain events (`student.admitted`, `enrollment.created`, `grade.finalized`, `payment.posted`, `hold.placed`) to HMAC-signed Standard Webhooks with retries, a dead-letter queue and replay. Contracts are OpenAPI 3.1 generated from code and tested in CI. Sanctum serves the first-party Inertia SPA, and Passport serves machine clients (client credentials) and delegated apps (PKCE). Token scopes intersect with the client's service-account role, tokens live ≤1 h, and integrations receive purpose-limited views; Moodle, for example, never sees financial or health data ([RFC 9457](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9457); [OpenAPI 3.1](https://spec.openapis.org/oas/v3.1.0); [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/); [RFC 9700](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9700)).

## Conclusion

The research changes the shape of the Phase 1 problem. The hard part of UMIS is not the module list, which every vendor draws the same way. It is the small number of decisions that cannot be undone later. The person/program/term keys are one. Others are whether curriculum versions and term types are effective-dated data, whether grades and money are append-only with reversals, and whether every Philippine rule lives in a policy table rather than in code. Get those right and later modules (degree-audit what-if, sponsor billing, LMS standards, verifiable credentials) become additions rather than migrations. The evidence also shows that Philippine practice is more varied than any single handbook suggests. Lapse windows, honors bands, unit loads, refund schedules and even the national rules on refunds and e-invoicing coverage are unsettled or school-specific. Configurability is therefore the safe default, not gold-plating.

The second implication concerns timing and governance. Two external clocks are already running. BIR e-invoicing coverage has to be determined before 31 Dec 2026, and every CHED/UniFAST cycle carries short statutory windows, such as the five-working-day Sec. 11 report. Both make the finance and reporting spine as urgent as the academic one. The failure cases show where projects actually break: calculations left outside the system, interfaces without owners, and untested enrollment peaks. Phase 1 planning should therefore budget explicitly for load testing, data migration (person records, curriculum history, AR balances) and a fit-to-standard review board, and should treat the verification register as a gating checklist for each module's design sign-off.
