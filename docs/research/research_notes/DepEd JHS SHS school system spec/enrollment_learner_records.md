# DepEd Enrollment, Learner Records and the Learner Information System (JHS Grades 7–10, SHS Grades 11–12), current to SY 2026–2027

Research date: 21 Nov 2026. Scope: one Philippine JHS–SHS school, configurable as public or private. Source quality: (P) = primary (DepEd central office issuance or LIS Support page). (D) = DepEd regional or division office reproducing or implementing a central issuance. (S) = secondary (teacher sites, slide decks, law-firm commentary, press). deped.gov.ph returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch, so several DepEd Orders were read through faithful reproductions. Those are marked.

**Headline change for the spec:** **DO 3, s. 2018 (Basic Education Enrollment Policy) is SUPERSEDED.** It was repealed by **DO 017, s. 2025, Revised Basic Education Enrollment Policy (13 June 2025)**, which applies from SY 2025–2026 onward and governs SY 2026–2027.

## 1. LRN and the Learner Information System (LIS): issuance, format, what the school encodes and when, and 2024–2026 changes

### Takeaway
The LRN is a permanent 12-digit number created in the LIS (DO 22, s. 2012). The school creates one only after the Division Planning Officer has verified that the learner has no existing LIS record. The LIS is the official enrollment record. The school works through a fixed annual cycle of LIS facilities: Early Registration, Quick Count, BOSY encoding (K–10 and SHS on separate windows), status updates during the year (transfers, dropouts), SHS semester enrollment, and EOSY status and tagging. Exact windows are announced each year on the LIS Support page and are extended often. For SY 2026–2027 the big change is that every Grade 11 learner is enrolled under the Strengthened SHS (SSHS) program, with elective tagging.

### Cited Findings
**LRN definition and format**
- The LRN is a "Twelve (12)-digit number which the pupil, student or learner shall keep while completing the basic education program, regardless of transfer to another school or learning center in the public or private sector, and promotion/moving up to the secondary level (DO 22, s. 2012)." — [DepEd Annex B, Modified School Forms data element descriptions (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf)
- DO 22, s. 2012, "Adoption of the Unique Learner Reference Number," is dated 20 March 2012. — [DepEd DO 22 s.2012 page (P, title only; fetch blocked)](https://deped.gov.ph/?p=8566)
- Format, partly verified: one example LRN, 301432080055, is broken down as the first 6 digits = School ID of the issuing school (301432) and the next 2 digits = school year (08). The last 4 digits are not explained; they appear to be a sequence number. — [LIS slide deck (S)](https://pt.slideshare.net/jaredram55/learner-information-system). The School ID is "a six (6) digit-number assigned to a school recognized in EBEIS." — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf)
- Uniqueness: before creating an LRN, the receiving adviser or ALS teacher asks the SDO Planning Officer to verify the learner's profile in the LIS so that no duplicate LRN is created. Learners with no record get an LRN. For learners from unregistered international schools, the receiving school grants the LRN on acceptance. — [DO 017 s.2025 Sec. IX.A.b, reproduced (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- Corrections to name, date of birth and sex go to SF1 through the LIS Learner Profile module. SF10 data must match SF1. — [DepEd PS slide deck on school forms (S)](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/preparation-and-checking-of-school-forms-sy2022-2023-as-of-june-22-2023-1-1-pptx/270583191)

**What the school encodes, and when (DO 017 s.2025, Sec. V.D and IX)**
- All early registration data goes into the LIS Early Registration Facility. Public schools encode daily, using the school head or administrator account. — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html). Advisers cannot use the Early Registration module. — [LIS Support, Previous Updates (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/previous-updates.html)
- The class adviser encodes BEEF data into the LIS. Public and private schools, SUCs/LUCs and Philippine Schools Overseas start BEEF encoding in the LIS BOSY module from the first week of classes. Enrollment status is updated from SF9 during BOSY. — [DO 017 s.2025 Sec. IX.A.b (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- "Enrollment in the LIS will continue for the rest of the school year, consistent with DepEd Order No. 17, s.2025." Schools are encouraged to encode as early as possible. — [PPS Advisory, 28 Jul 2025 (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/Manuals/ADVISORY-SHS-Pilot.pdf)
- Transfers in and out: incoming learners are tagged "transferred in" and departing learners "transferred out." The transfer-in/out window runs from the 1st Friday of the school calendar to the last day of Q3. Transfer out is evidenced by a request for SF10. Special cases such as migration or disasters may transfer at any time. — [DO 017 s.2025 Sec. IV(s)(t), IX (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- LIS transfer mechanics: a transfer is a request from the receiving school that the originating school confirms or declines. If a transfer is not confirmed by EOSY, the LIS tags the learner as a Dropout at the originating school, and the learner is not listed in the receiving school's masterlist. — [LIS Support, Previous Updates (P, older guidance, still describes the mechanism)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/previous-updates.html). Enrolling the learner in the LIS notifies the originating school, so a written transfer request is no longer needed where LIS notification is available. — [DepEd PS slide deck (S)](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/pspoliciesonenrolmenttransferofdocscheckingofschoolformsandsf10asofmay2022pptx/252186313)
- Learner statuses and terms in the LIS: "No Longer in School (NLS)" was renamed "No Longer Participating in Learning Session (NLPLS)" and then "No Longer Participating in Learning Activities." The EOSY 2024–2025 update added an "Updated list of Reasons for learners with No Longer in School status" and required "SHS Exit Choices" tagging for Grade 12. "Date of Official Enrolment" was relabelled "Date of First Attendance" (Aug 2022). — [LIS Support bulletin (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)
- Tagging facilities the school maintains: ADM, BMI, SBFP, ESC and SHS QVR/voucher (private schools), CCT/4Ps recipient with 4Ps Household ID (minimum 12 characters), learners with disabilities (classification/type), IP ethnicity, Honors (Grades 1–10), SHS exit choices, ARAL learners (2026), and SSHS electives. Learner Quarterly Grades and NAT integration were added in Feb 2023. — [LIS Support bulletin (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)

**SY 2025–2026 to SY 2026–2027 LIS calendar, as announced (LIS Support bulletin, P)** — [LIS Support](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)
- 27 Feb 2026: early registration encoding for incoming K, G1, G7 and G11 extended to 14 Mar 2026, later 19 Mar 2026. Grade 11 learners already encoded had to be **re-encoded under Strengthened SHS**.
- 16 Mar 2026: EOSY Updating SY 2025–2026 opened until 31 May 2026, then extended to 15 Jun 2026. ARAL tagging was required (OM-OASSM-2026-03-123), and SSHS elective tagging had to be completed during EOSY.
- 4 Jun 2026: EBEIS SY 2026–2027 and **LIS Quick Count** opened. Schools encode **daily enrollment by grade level and sex** from 4–30 Jun 2026, extended to 12 Jul 2026. SSHS Quick Count reports Academic under GAS and Tech-Pro under TVL.
- 13 Jul 2026: **BOSY encoding K–10 open 13 Jul–30 Sep 2026**, later extended. From 14 Jul, ADM/SBFP/BMI/ESC tagging.
- 13 Aug 2026 enhancements: Enrollment Search shows a learner's 3-SY enrollment history. The LSEN manifestation choice was renamed "Difficulty in Basic Learning and Applying Knowledge."
- 30 Sep 2026: **SHS Enrollment Facility BOSY 2026–2027 open 30 Sep–30 Nov 2026.** "All Grade 11 learners shall be enrolled under the Strengthened Senior High School (SSHS) program, including Grade 12 learners under the SSHS pilot schools," while other Grade 12 learners stay in the regular K-12 SHS program. Private schools and SLUCs must set their School Calendar in the LIS before creating classes. Public school heads create SHS Adviser accounts. "K to 10 BOSY 2026-2027 encoding is extended, and K to 12 enrollment data will be generated on November 30, 2026."
- A division memo states that the LIS "shall serve as the official enrolment" for SY 2026–2027 and that BOSY encoding runs 13 Jul–30 Sep 2026, citing a central memo of 7 Jul 2026. — [SDO Dasmariñas DM 547 s.2026 (D)](https://www.depeddasma.edu.ph/dm-no-547-s-2026-encoding-of-the-learner-information-system-lis-for-beginning-of-school-year-bosy-2026-2027/)
- Schools that did not reach 100% of the previous SY's enrollment in Quick Count had to submit a justification report (Muntinlupa: by 20 Jul 2026). — [SDO Muntinlupa UN-2026-264 (D)](https://deped-muntinlupa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/UN-2026-264.pdf)
- SSHS pilot (SY 2025–2026): 891 pilot schools were tagged in EBEIS as offering "Academic Track (Strengthened)" and/or "Technical Professional," with Grade 11 sections created under "(Strengthened SHS) Academic Track" / "(Strengthened SHS) Technical-Professional (TechPro) Track." — [PPS Advisory 28 Jul 2025 (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/Manuals/ADVISORY-SHS-Pilot.pdf)
- SY 2026–2027 calendar: DO 009, s. 2026 sets a three-term calendar, with classes from 8 Jun 2026 to 8 Apr 2027. Private schools are not required to adopt the three-term calendar. — [Malaya / explained.ph via search (S)](https://malaya.com.ph/news/national-news/deped-announces-june-8-opening-for-sy-2026-2027/)

### Inferences
- The LRN should be stored as a 12-character string with a checked format (digits only, 12 long), not as an integer. The system must never generate LRNs. It records the LRN assigned in the LIS, or marks the learner as "LRN pending, new record awaiting LIS creation."
- The LIS is the system of record, and our system is a local mirror. The module needs: (a) an export or worksheet that matches LIS BOSY fields; (b) per-SY configurable LIS windows (Early Reg, Quick Count, BOSY K–10, SHS BOSY, SHS 2nd sem, EOSY), because dates move every year; (c) a Quick Count daily tally by grade and sex; (d) status codes that mirror LIS (Enrolled, Temporarily Enrolled, Transferred In, Transferred Out, Dropped/NLPLA with reason, Promoted, Retained/Irregular, Completed/Graduated).
- With the three-term calendar for public schools against an optional calendar for private schools, the term structure has to be configurable. SHS also keeps semesters in the LIS (SHS 1st sem end status and 2nd sem enrollment).
- SHS program modelling must carry both the legacy K-12 SHS (Track > Strand) and the SSHS model (Academic / TechPro track > elective cluster > electives) through SY 2026–2027. The legacy model is needed because Grade 12 in non-pilot schools is still on it.

### Gaps
- There is no official digit-by-digit LRN specification. The meaning of the last 4 digits is unverified, and the DO 22 s.2012 text was not retrievable (403).
- No "LIS version number" is published. No official "LIS 2.0" or full replacement system was found for 2024–2026, only incremental enhancements. A May–June 2024 "system upgrade" outage of LIS/EBEIS/BEIS was announced (Memo OUA-OUT-052724-5). — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)
- The LIS BOSY field list (screen-level) was not retrieved. User guides are linked from the LIS Support Manuals tab.

## 2. Enrollment policy: governing orders, BEEF, early registration, documents, age, transferees, balik-aral, LWD, IP, 4Ps

### Takeaway
DO 017, s. 2025 governs enrollment for SY 2025–2026 onward and repealed DO 3, s. 2018. Its key rules are:
- Early registration runs from the last Saturday of January to the last Friday of February, for incoming K/G1/G7/G11. Learners moving up within a school (G8–10, G12) count as pre-registered.
- Enrollment opens one week before classes start, and it continues through the year.
- The birth certificate is submitted **once** in all of K–12. Secondary documents are accepted until **31 October**.
- Grade 7, 8–10, 11 and 12 entrants need the prior grade's **SF9**.
- No fees may be collected as a condition of enrolling.
- Transferees from private schools with unpaid accounts are accepted as **Temporarily Enrolled** on an Affidavit of Undertaking.

### Cited Findings
**Superseded and current orders**
- DO 017, s. 2025 "repeals DepEd Order No. 03, s. 2018 titled Basic Education Enrollment Policy." It covers public schools, private schools and ALS CLCs for SY 2025–2026 and subsequent SYs. Private schools and SUCs/LUCs "may impose other admission conditions." — [DO 017 s.2025 reproduced (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html); confirmed by [LIS PPS Advisory referencing DO 17 s.2025 (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/Manuals/ADVISORY-SHS-Pilot.pdf) and [DepEd Memorandum 023 s.2026 (P, via SDO Muntinlupa)](https://deped-muntinlupa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NUM-2026-135.pdf)
- Superseded: DO 3 s.2018 (and its Annex 3 Affidavit of Undertaking form, which is still hosted on the LIS support site). DO 35 s.2022 restored in-person enrollment under DO 3. DM 032 s.2024 contained the SY 2024–2025 enrollment guidelines. — [jur.ph summary (S)](https://jur.ph/law/summary/basic-education-enrollment-policy), [DepEd DM 032 s.2024 page (P title)](https://www.deped.gov.ph/2024/06/26/june-25-2024-dm-032-s-2024-enrollment-guidelines-for-school-year-2024-2025/)
- In SY 2025–2026 the public school enrollment week was 9–13 June 2025. — [Manila Bulletin (S)](https://mb.com.ph/2025/06/05/deped-sets-nationwide-public-school-enrollment-period-from-june-9-to-13)

**Early registration (Sec. V)**
- Covers incoming Kindergarten, Grades 1, 7 and 11, transferees into public schools, and first-time ALS learners. It "does not guarantee official enrollment." The Basic Education Early Registration Form is Annex A. — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- Schedule: last Saturday of January to last Friday of February. "Incoming Grades 2–6, 8–10, and 12 are considered pre-registered." Documents are shown for verification only and are not collected. — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- SY 2026–2027 early registration ran 31 Jan–27 Feb 2026. Private schools are encouraged but not required to run it. — [SDO Muntinlupa NUM 2026-012 (D)](https://deped-muntinlupa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NUM-2026-012.pdf); [PIA NCR (S)](https://pia-ncr.blogspot.com/2026/02/deped-starts-early-registration-for.html). LIS encoding was extended to 19 Mar 2026. — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)
- Advocacy must target learners with disabilities, GIDA and off-grid communities, disaster-displaced families, children in conflict with the law or in armed conflict, learners with chronic illness, stateless or undocumented persons, and out-of-school learners. — [DO 017 s.2025 Sec. V.B (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)

**Enrollment eligibility for JHS/SHS (Sec. VI.B)** — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- Grade 7: Grade 6 graduate with Grade 6 SF9, or PEPT/A&E Elementary passer with Certificate of Rating or PPA (ALS Portfolio) certificate.
- Grades 8–10 and 12: completion of the last grade attended, plus that grade's SF9.
- Grade 11: Grade 10 completer with Grade 10 SF9, or PEPT/A&E JHS passer with Certificate of Rating or PPA certificate.
- Balik-Aral (a learner who resumes after at least 1 year of dropping out): PEPT/A&E passer or prior SF9/SF10, plus a birth certificate or secondary document.
- Late enrollment (Sec. VI.D): accepted if the learner can still meet 80% of prescribed school days and the quarterly requirements. Otherwise acceptance is at the school head's discretion, with catch-up interventions.
- No fees (Sec. VI.C): no voluntary fees may be collected as a prerequisite during early registration or enrollment (cf. DO 19 s.2008). Forms are not for sale.

**Documentary requirements (Sec. VII)** — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- The PSA/NSO birth certificate is submitted **once** in K–12. — corroborated by [GMA News (S)](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/949408/deped-submit-once-child-birth-certificate-entire-k-12/story)
- Accepted secondary documents until the PSA copy is available, up to **31 October** of the SY: National ID or primary government ID; LCR Certificate of Live Birth; marriage certificate; PhilHealth ID; PWD ID; barangay certification (name, birth date, sex, parents); affidavit of undertaking by parents; Certificate of Foundling; baptismal certificate.
- Transferees from PH public or private schools submit SF9 signed by the School Head, or a registrar's letter certifying the last grade completed.
- Special cases: learners from international schools (SF9 or equivalent, no PEPT); Philippine schools overseas or foreign schools (apostilled records, PEPT where needed, cf. DO 26 s.1994); unrecognized schools (PEPT certificate of rating); learners displaced by calamity or conflict (proof of identity, with the SDO checking the LIS for an existing record).

**Enrollment modalities and BEEF (Sec. VIII)** — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- In-person enrollment is preferred. For minors, the parent or guardian completes the **Basic Education Enrollment Form (BEEF, Annex B)**. A minor may submit the form if a parent or guardian signs it. Learners of legal age enroll themselves.
- Incoming G7 and G11 (and K, G1): the parent or guardian reaffirms the intent to enroll. G8–10 and G12 learners with existing LRNs submit only a **Confirmation Slip (Annex C)**.
- Remote options: online (BEEF sent to the school's official email or messaging channel) and dropbox. The School Enrollment Focal Person collects the forms and passes them to grade coordinators, then to advisers, who encode them in the LIS BOSY facility.
- BEEF fields: the DO 017 Annex B text was not retrieved. Earlier BEEF versions (the "Enhanced BEEF," posted 25 Jul 2022, and the "Modified BEEF," 2021) are on the LIS Support page. — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/). LIS learner-profile fields evidenced by enhancements include email address, current and permanent residence with ZIP, country of citizenship, actual modality, CCT/4Ps Household ID, IP ethnicity, and disability classification/type. — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)

**Transfers and private-school arrears (Sec. IX)**
- A learner from a private school with unsettled accounts is accepted by the public school, which helps the parent sign an **affidavit of undertaking**. The private school has **30 calendar days** from electronic notice through LIS Tracking/Transfer to confirm, after which the learner is tagged temporarily enrolled. — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- "Temporarily enrolled" means a learner not officially enrolled because documentary requirements are missing. — [DO 017 s.2025 Sec. IV(r) (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- ALS-to-formal and formal-to-ALS: the learner is enrolled in the LIS once attendance begins. An LIS facility exists for temporary enrollment of G7 and G11 learners coming from ALS. — [DO 017 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html); [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)

**Learner groups**
- IP learner: born to one or both IP parents, as defined in RA 8371. — [DO 017 Sec. IV(g) (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html). The LIS has an "Updating of Ethnicity for IP learners facility (based on the updated list)" (Oct 2023). — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/). SF1 carries "IP (Specify ethnic group)", "Mother Tongue" and "Religion (Islam for Muslim learners)." — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf)
- Learners with disabilities and SNED: the PWD ID or medical evaluation is optional ("if available"). The LIS records disability classification/type and the LSEN manifestation (renamed Aug 2026). — [DO 017 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html); [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)
- 4Ps: DO 017 has no specific 4Ps enrollment rule. The LIS has "Tagging of Conditional Cash Transfer (4Ps)," including for private schools, and a "4Ps Household ID No" field with a minimum of 12 characters. — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)
- Private SHS subsidies: SHS Voucher Program redemption for SY 2026–2027 runs 3 Aug–3 Nov 2026. ALS A&E passers from poor to middle-income households apply directly at the voucher-participating SHS during enrollment rather than through OVAP (DM 031 s.2026). — SDO Muntinlupa Memorandum No. 223 s.2026, which transmits DM 031 s.2026 dated 18 May 2026 (D). The PDF was read from a copy downloaded alongside this research. **Its exact URL was not confirmed, so treat this as UNVERIFIED until it is checked on deped-muntinlupa.com.**. ESC and SHS QVR/voucher IDs are tagged in the LIS. — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)

**Age**
- SF1 records "Age by June (1st Friday)." — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf). Later, the LIS displayed age as of 31 Oct. — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)
- DO 017 sets no minimum or maximum age for Grades 7–12 in the text retrieved. Only K and SNED age rules appear (SNED: at least 5 years old at the start of the SY). — [DO 017 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)

### Inferences
- Enrollment workflow states: Early-registered → Pre-registered (continuing learners) → Enrolled (BEEF or Confirmation Slip received, LIS encoded) or Temporarily Enrolled (missing SF9, PSA copy or credentials) → official. Each document needs a checklist with a due date. The PSA birth certificate is due by 31 Oct when only a secondary document was given. For transfer credentials, records must be secured within 30 days of first attendance.
- Continuing learners (G8–10, G12) only need a confirmation slip, which suits a one-click "re-enroll" flow on a parent portal.
- Private-school mode needs: tuition and assessment hooks, ESC and SHS voucher fields, admission conditions such as entrance tests (Sec. II allows these), and its own school calendar.
- Public-school mode must block any fee as a prerequisite to enrollment.
- The BEEF capture form should be versioned, because DepEd revises it by memo (Sec. VIII says form revisions come by memorandum).

### Gaps
- The DO 017 s.2025 Annex A (Early Registration Form), Annex B (BEEF) and Annex C (Confirmation Slip) field lists were not retrieved. The DO text was read through a blog reproduction with OCR errors ("Batik-Aral" for Balik-Aral). Verify against the deped.gov.ph PDF.
- No national DepEd Memorandum fixing the SY 2026–2027 regular enrollment week was found. One calendar summary lists 1–5 Jun 2026, and a blog lists 4–29 May 2026. Both are unverified.
- No DO 017 provisions specific to Muslim learners or 4Ps were found. Age-eligibility rules for G7/G11 are not stated.

## 3. School Forms SF1–SF10: purpose, fields, and which the LIS generates

### Takeaway
DO 4, s. 2014 adopted SF1–SF7, replacing 16 older forms. DO 58, s. 2017 added SF8 (health and nutrition), SF9 (progress report, ES/JHS/SHS) and SF10 (permanent record, ES/JHS/SHS), plus SHS-specific SF5A/SF5B, and standardized permanent records. The LIS generates SF1–SF6 from encoded data; SF1, SF2, SF3 and SF5 have been updated since 2022. **SF9 and SF10 are still prepared by the school**, using official Excel templates and e-class records from the LIS Support Downloads, including a new SSHS SF10 and ECR in 2026. Forms from unofficial sources are not valid.

### Cited Findings
- DO 4 s.2014, Annex A list: SF1 School Register; SF2 Daily Attendance Report of Learners; SF3 Books Issued and Returned; SF4 Monthly Learners' Movement and Attendance; SF5 Report on Promotion and Level of Proficiency; SF6 Summarized Report on Promotion and Level of Proficiency; SF7 School Personnel Assignment List and Basic Profile. — [DepEd Annex A (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf). All 7 replaced 16 earlier forms from SY 2014–2015. — [teacherph (S)](https://www.teacherph.com/adoption-modified-school-forms-sfs-public-elementary-secondary-schools/)
- **SF1 (BOSY and EOSY)** fields: School ID, SY, school name, grade level, section, LRN, learner name (as on birth certificate), sex, birth date, place of birth (province), age by 1st Friday of June, mother tongue, IP group, religion, house/street, barangay, municipality, province, father's name, mother's name, guardian name, relationship, parent/guardian contact number, remarks. — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf)
- **SF2 (updated daily, submitted by the 5th of the following month)**: daily attendance per learner, days absent and tardy, enrolment as of 1st Friday of June, late enrolment, registered learners at month end, % enrolment, average daily attendance, % attendance, learners with 5 consecutive absences (the basis for a home visit), dropouts, transferred in and out (M/F/Total). — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf)
- **SF3 (BOSY/EOSY)**: book title and subject, date issued, date returned. Loss codes include FM = Force Majeure, TDO = Transferred/Dropout, NEG = negligence. Action codes: LLTR (letter from learner signed by parent), TLTR (teacher report), PTL (paid by learner). — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf)
- **SF4 (by the 10th of the following month, lifted from SF2)**: registered learners, ADA, % attendance, dropouts, transferred out and in (previous month cumulative, this month, cumulative). — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf)
- **SF5 (EOSY)**: promotion status and level of proficiency per learner, with LRN, used for all grades. SF6 summarizes SF5. — [Annex B (P)](https://rtei.okfn.org/documents/List_of_Modified_School_Forms.pdf). ## 4. Releasing SF9/SF10 and records, withholding for unpaid fees, certificates

### Takeaway
Records move school-to-school only: SF9, SF10 and the PSA birth certificate are sent by authorized personnel, and learners and parents may not be made to carry them. Records must move before the end of the first grading period, or within 30 days for mid-year transfers. Scanned SF9/SF10 sent by official email is allowed for G6→G7 and G10→G11.

**Withholding for unpaid fees (private schools):** DepEd Memorandum 023, s. 2026 (27 Mar 2026) confirms that private schools may lawfully withhold official transfer credentials until debts are settled, under DO 88 s.2010. In that case the receiving school:
- tags the learner Temporarily Enrolled on an Affidavit of Undertaking;
- must not officially promote or graduate the learner in DepEd systems, or issue official permanent records or final progress reports, until the original credentials arrive;
- must still let the learner join EOSY rites.

Only SHS completers receive a diploma and graduation rites. Grade 10 (JHS) completers get a certificate and a moving-up ceremony.

### Cited Findings
- Sec. IX.B of DO 017 — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html):
  - "Only schools and authorized personnel transmit SF 9, SF 10, and PSA/NSO birth certificates… Learners and parents may not be compelled to carry records."
  - Records transfer before the end of the first grading period. Mid-year transfers secure records within 30 days of first attendance (DO 54 s.2016).
  - Scanned SF9/SF10 may be sent by official email if requested through LIS Tracking/Transfer, for G6 graduates in G7 and G10 completers in G11. "Receiving schools cannot require physical copies."
- DO 54, s. 2016 "Guidelines on the Request and Transfer of Learner's School Records" sets standard processes for requesting and releasing Form 137 and Form 138. — [DepEd page (P title)](https://deped.gov.ph/?p=3633); [teacherph (S)](https://www.teacherph.com/guidelines-request-transfer-learners-school-records/)
- LIS rule: "The originating school is obliged to send their former learner's documents to the receiving school as soon as a request is made, or at least respond to such request, regardless of how it was communicated." When declining, leave the reason in Remarks. — [LIS Support, Previous Updates (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/previous-updates.html)
- **DepEd Memorandum No. 023, s. 2026 (27 Mar 2026)**, "Clarification on the Participation of Temporarily Enrolled Learners in EOSY Rites," — [full text via SDO Muntinlupa NUM-2026-135 (P reproduced)](https://deped-muntinlupa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NUM-2026-135.pdf):
  - (2a) "No learner shall be denied participation in EOSY rites on the basis of unsettled financial or property obligations."
  - (2b) Transferees from private schools without credentials because of unsettled obligations are tagged Temporarily Enrolled, consistent with DO 017 s.2025.
  - (2c) "Under DO 88, s. 2010 (2010 Revised Manual of Regulations for Private Schools in Basic Education), private schools maintain the legal right to withhold official transfer credentials until all such debts are fully settled."
  - (2d) The Affidavit of Undertaking is reiterated as "a binding guarantee of future payment."
  - (3) Until complete credentials arrive, the learner "cannot be officially promoted to a higher grade level nor officially graduate in the Department's systems," and receiving schools are "strictly prohibited from issuing official permanent records or final progress reports."
  - (4) Official confirmation of graduation status comes only after payment or a settlement.
  - (5) "Schools are mandated to release credentials immediately once all obligations are satisfied."
- Commentary links the withholding right to Sec. 128 of the 2010 Revised Manual (DO 88 s.2010). The exact text was not verified. — [Respicio & Co. (S)](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/can-a-private-school-withhold-report-cards-for-unpaid-tuition)
- Limits: no interest may be charged on unpaid tuition unless the enrollment contract expressly provides for it (DO 63 s.1999). — [DepEd DO 63 s.1999 page (P title)](https://deped.gov.ph/?p=6134). Withholding SF9 over non-compulsory voluntary or PTA contributions is hard to justify. — [Respicio (S)](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/can-a-school-withhold-student-records-over-unpaid-contributions)
- Correction to a common belief: there is **no blanket DepEd prohibition** on private schools withholding records for unpaid tuition. The rule is withhold-but-accommodate. A 2019 DepEd statement waiving "financial clearance" for transfers to public schools drew objections from private schools. — [Manila Bulletin 2019 (S)](https://mb.com.ph/2019/2/5/private-schools-decry-deped-statement-waiving-financial-clearance-requirement); superseded in practice by DM 023 s.2026 above.
- Certificates and rites: only SHS finishers have graduation rites and receive diplomas. K, Grade 6 and Grade 10 finishers have completion or moving-up ceremonies and receive a certificate. — [GMA News (S)](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/news/nation/686621/deped-limits-diplomas-graduation-rites-to-grade-12-finishers/story/); [ABS-CBN (S)](https://www.abs-cbn.com/news/03/01/19/graduation-rites-for-senior-high-students-only-says-deped). DM 015 s.2026 (SY 2025–2026 EOSY rites) bars collecting contributions for rites and making field trips or proms a completion requirement. — [Manila Bulletin (S)](https://mb.com.ph/2026/03/06/simple-safe-and-meaningful-deped-issues-reminders-for-2026-end-of-school-year-rites); [BusinessWorld (S)](https://www.bworldonline.com/education/2026/03/24/738248/deped-field-trip-prom-not-required-for-graduation/)
- LIS tagging of Honors for Grades 1–10 is required, as are SHS Exit Choices for Grade 12. — [LIS Support (P)](https://support.lis.deped.gov.ph/support/)

### Inferences
- Records-release module: an outbound request queue keyed to LIS transfer requests, with SLA timers (end of Q1, or 30 days after first attendance for mid-year), dispatch method (courier, official email scan for G7/G11), and a certified-true-copy log. In private mode, add a "hold for financial obligations" flag that blocks release of the original SF9/SF10, diploma or certificate.
- The finance hold must never block: enrollment elsewhere, the learner's EOSY rites participation, or response to LIS transfer notices (which must at least be answered). In public mode the hold flag should be disabled.
- For a Temporarily Enrolled learner the system must block: promotion or graduation finalization, final SF9, SF10, the Grade 10 certificate and the SHS diploma, until the original credentials are received. Rites eligibility stays allowed.
- Certificate templates: Certificate of Enrollment, Good Moral Character, Grade 10 Certificate of Completion (JHS), SHS Diploma, plus Form 137 request letters. Number each issuance and log it.

### Gaps
- No primary DepEd order prescribing the format of the Good Moral Certificate or Certificate of Enrollment was found. These appear to be school-issued and unstandardized; this is unverified. The current Grade 10 certificate and SHS diploma templates (the EOSY rites DO/DM annexes, e.g. DO 2 s.2019 / DO 2 s.2020) were not retrieved.
- The DO 88 s.2010 Sec. 128 text was not verified. Whether a DO 88 replacement (an updated Manual of Regulations for Private Schools) exists by 2026 was not checked.
- Whether records fees (for certified true copies) may be charged by public schools: not found.

## 5. Data Privacy Act obligations for minors' data in DepEd

### Takeaway
DO 017 s.2025 Sec. X sets the enrollment-specific privacy rules:
- collect data only for enrollment and related policy purposes;
- restrict access to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis;
- no disclosure of personally identifiable information, including on social media, without explicit consent of the learner **and** parent/guardian, unless the law requires it;
- **destroy BEEF and confirmation slips after one year** under DepEd records management.

DepEd has no single learner-privacy DepEd Order that could be found. The rest of the framework is RA 10173 and its IRR, NPC issuances, the Child Protection Policy (DO 40 s.2012) for images and posts, and DepEd privacy notices pointing to its DPO.

### Cited Findings
- DO 017 s.2025 Sec. X — [DO 017 s.2025 (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html):
  - purpose limitation;
  - technical and organizational security measures;
  - need-to-know access;
  - no PII disclosure to third parties "including on social media, without explicit consent of the learner and parent/guardian, unless required by law or necessary for legitimate controller purposes";
  - BEEF and confirmation slips "may not be kept longer than one year, after which they are destroyed";
  - cites RA 10173, its IRR and NPC issuances.
  Secondary documents collected at enrollment must also be handled under these provisions (Sec. VII).
- Schools must comply with the Data Privacy Act and with RA 9470 (National Archives Act) records rules. — [DO 017 Sec. XI (D/S)](https://eduknasyon.blogspot.com/2025/06/deped-order-017-s-2025-revised-basic.html)
- DepEd privacy notice: data subjects may request access, correction or deletion. Contact: dataprivacy.dpo@deped.gov.ph. — [SDO Naga privacy notice (D)](https://www.depednaga.ph/?p=7149)
- Learners' names, sections, addresses and other personal information must not be posted publicly online without prior parental consent. Use Google Classroom, Moodle or DepEd email for grades. — [Region 4-A / division memo via search (D)](https://www.depeddasma.edu.ph/?p=15171)
- Posting learner images or data requires prior consent of a parent or guardian, citing DO 40 s.2012 (Child Protection Policy), with a consent and waiver form. — [SDO Tangub DM 050 s.2025 (D)](https://tangub.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/divmemo050_2025.pdf)
- NPC PHE Bulletin No. 16 (online K-12 classes): obtain consent for recordings, and for minors the parent or guardian's explicit consent before online proctoring. — [NPC (P)](https://privacy.gov.ph/?p=11354)
- DO 003, s. 2026 (AI guidelines) requires a Privacy Impact Assessment before any AI system is deployed, covering data privacy, security and child safety. — [DM 130 s.2026 citing DO 003 s.2026 (D)](https://www.depedimuscity.com/portal/uploads/DM_130_S_2026.pdf)
- Requests for sensitive personal information held by DepEd are referred to the Office of the Secretary/DPO (DO 002 s.2019, per a division memo). Information requests generally follow the DepEd FOI Manual (DO 72 s.2016). — [search summary of division notices (D)](https://www.depednaga.ph/?p=7149). Template notices conflict on what DO 72 s.2016 is; it is most likely the FOI Manual.

### Inferences
- Treat as sensitive personal information (RA 10173): disability, health and BMI (SF8), religion, IP ethnicity, 4Ps status, and birth certificate data. Apply field-level access control (adviser, registrar, guidance, nurse) and audit logs.
- Consent model: a parent/guardian signature captured with the BEEF. Separate opt-in consents for photo or name publication (honors lists, social media) and for any AI processing.
- Retention policy: purge BEEF and confirmation-slip images or scans after 1 year. SF10 is a permanent record and SF9 is the learner's copy. Copies of the birth certificate follow the permanent file (DO 58 says the birth certificate is attached to the learner's permanent record; [slide deck (S)](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/preparation-and-checking-of-school-forms-sy2022-2023-as-of-june-22-2023-1-1-pptx/270583191)).
- Private schools are personal information controllers in their own right and need their own DPO registration and PIA under NPC rules. The DepEd DPO covers public schools. This is inference; confirm against NPC registration circulars.

### Gaps
- No central DepEd Order or DepEd Privacy Manual specific to learner data was located. No DepEd-specific age-of-consent rule was found; under PH law minors act through parents or guardians. DepEd records retention schedules (the NAP General Records Disposition Schedule applied to SF1–SF10) were not retrieved.
- NPC circulars on registration and DPO requirements for private schools, and the NPC 2023–2025 education-sector guidance, were not researched in depth.
