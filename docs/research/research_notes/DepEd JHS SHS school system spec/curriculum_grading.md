# DepEd JHS (Grades 7–10) and SHS (Grades 11–12): Curriculum, Grading, Promotion and Recognition Rules (as of SY 2026–2027)

> Research date: 2026-11-21. **Headline finding: DepEd Order No. 015, s. 2026 (signed 4 June 2026, effective SY 2026–2027) repeals DO 8 s.2015 (classroom assessment) and DO 36 s.2016 (awards).** The 2015/2016 rules are now *legacy*. They still apply to Grade 12 learners who are not on the Strengthened SHS Curriculum in SY 2026–2027, and they are needed to import historical records. A grading engine needs at least three rule sets: (a) DO 8/DO 36 legacy, (b) DO 15 transition year SY 2026–27 (adjusted transmutation), (c) DO 15 zero-based from SY 2027–28.

## 1. MATATAG curriculum (K–10): rollout and JHS learning areas and time allotments

### Takeaway
MATATAG is phased in by grade. Grade 7 started in SY 2024–25 and Grade 8 in SY 2025–26. Grade 9 starts in SY 2026–27. Grade 10 is in SY 2026–27 under DO 10 s.2024, but the original 2023 plan put it in SY 2027–28, so this is unresolved. JHS has 8 learning areas, each by default 45 min × 5 days. A 2024 amendment allows flexible slots, with weekly minimums of 225 min (English, Math, Science, GMRC/VE) and 200 min (Filipino, AP, MAPEH, TLE).

### Cited Findings
- DO 10 s.2024 (Policy Guidelines on the Implementation of the MATATAG Curriculum) sets phased rollout. It starts with Kindergarten and Grades 1, 4 and 7 in SY 2024–2025 — [Manila Bulletin, 28 Jul 2024](https://mb.com.ph/2024/7/28/dep-ed-releases-policy-guidelines-for-matatag-curriculum-implementation-for-sy-2024-2025); [DepEdClub summary of DO 10 s.2024 (secondary)](https://depedclub.com/policy-guidelines-on-the-implementation-of-the-matatag-curriculum-d-o-no-010-s-2024/)

Grade 10 is in SY 2027–28 — [PNA](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1207588); [Cebu Daily News](https://cebudailynews.inquirer.net/521256/deped-launches-recalibrated-k-10-curriculum-to-start-in-sy-2024-2025)
- **Conflict:** summaries of DO 10 s.2024 list Grades 6, 9 **and 10** in SY 2026–27. I did not verify this against the signed order. **UNVERIFIED.**
- In DO 15 s.2026, Key Stage 3 = Grades 7–10 (JHS) and Key Stage 4 = Grades 11–12 (SHS) — [DepEdClub on DO 15 s.2026](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/)
- JHS learning areas (MATATAG, Grades 7–10): Filipino, English, Mathematics, Science, Araling Panlipunan (AP), Values Education (VE), MAPEH, and TLE. The base schedule is **45 min, 5×/week each**, plus a 30-min National Mathematics/Reading Program (NMP) slot 4×/week and a 45-min Homeroom Guidance (HGP) slot 1×/week. The total is 390 min/day Mon–Thu and 405 min Fri — DepEd-sourced table summarized in search results, [Muntinlupa SDO memo NUM-2026-190](https://deped-muntinlupa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/NUM-2026-190.pdf); [DepEd MATATAG FAQ](https://deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/FAQs-ON-THE-MATATAG-CURRICULUM.pdf)

  - uniform 50-, 55- or 60-minute slots per learning area, 5×/week, with Homeroom 45 min 1×/week;
  - other combinations, as long as learning contact time is **≥ 5 h 30 min/day**;
  - weekly minimums: **English, Mathematics, Science, GMRC/VE ≥ 225 min/week**; **EPP/TLE, MAPEH, AP, Filipino ≥ 200 min/week**.
  - Sources: [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1985106/schools-given-more-flexibility-for-matatag-curriculum); [Manila Bulletin, 19 Sep 2024](https://mb.com.ph/2024/9/19/flexible-matatag-curriculum-guidelines-set-for-implementation-in-q2-of-sy-2024-2025-dep-ed); [DZRH](https://www.dzrh.com.ph/post/deped-amends-policy-guidelines-for-matatag-curriculum-gives-flexibility-on-school-work-hours)
- SY 2026–27 uses a **three-term calendar** (DO 009 s.2026, signed 16 Apr 2026). Classes run from 8 June 2026 to 8 April 2027. Public schools must adopt it. Private schools may adopt it if they meet the required school days. ### Inferences
- For SY 2026–27: Grades 7, 8 and 9 are MATATAG. Grade 10 is probably MATATAG under DO 10 s.2024, but configure it per school. Set "MATATAG start SY" as a per-grade-level setting rather than hard-coding it.
- The grading period is now **Term (3/year)**, not Quarter (4/year). The engine must support both: quarters for legacy years up to SY 2025–26, terms from SY 2026–27. Store the period count per school year.
- The time-allotment model should store minutes/session × sessions/week and validate against the weekly minimums above. The DepEd defaults are 225 and 200.
- "Values Education" (Grades 7–10) is the JHS name for GMRC. EsP is the legacy (K to 12 / DO 8) name.

## 2. Senior High School: tracks, strands, Strengthened SHS Curriculum

### Takeaway
SY 2026–27 has two SHS curricula running side by side:
- **Grade 11:** Strengthened SHS Curriculum, fully implemented. It was piloted in Grade 11 in SY 2025–26 under DM 48 s.2025.
- **Grade 12:** mostly the legacy K to 12 track/strand curriculum.

The strengthened curriculum has **2 tracks (Academic, TechPro)**, **5 core subjects (160 h each, Grade 11)** and elective clusters. Academic electives are 80 h/semester and TechPro electives are 320 h/year. The first full Grade 12 cohort is in SY 2027–28.

### Inferences
- The subject master should carry a `subject_group` / `grading_category` enum. The DO 15 SHS groups above drive weights. Separately, store the legacy `core|applied|specialized` type and track/strand for Grade 12 legacy and historical records.
- Store hours per subject (160 core, 80 academic elective, 320 TechPro elective). DO 15 says SHS general average uses "equivalent units per subject", which implies hours-weighted averaging.
- Grade 12 in SY 2026–27 needs the legacy curriculum + DO 8 weights + adjusted transmutation table (§3). Pilot-school Grade 12 learners need the strengthened curriculum, so the curriculum version must be set per learner or section, not per grade.

## 3. Grading: DO 8 s.2015 (legacy) and DO 15 s.2026 (current)

### Takeaway
The computation pipeline is the same in both orders:
1. raw scores
2. percentage score
3. weighted score
4. initial grade
5. transmuted grade, or none from SY 2027–28

What changes in DO 15:
- **Components are renamed:** Written/Oral Works (WW), Product/Performance Tasks (PT), Examinations (EX).
- **Weights change.**
- **Periods change:** terms replace quarters.
- **SY 2026–27 uses an adjusted transmutation table** where IG 70 → 75 (the old table had IG 60 → 75).
- **From SY 2027–28 there is no transmutation** (zero-based grading, report-card floor 60).
- **Descriptors are renamed** to Advancing / Benchmarking / Connecting / Developing / Emerging.
- **Formative assessments no longer count** toward grades.

### Cited Findings — DO 15 s.2026 (current)
- **Identity:**
  - Title: "Revised Guidelines on Classroom Assessment, Grading System, and Awards and Recognition for the K to 12 Basic Education Program".

- **Zero-based grading (SY 2027–28 onward, Grades 4–12):** no transmutation. Term Grade = ΣWS (rounded). The default minimum on the report card is 60. ### Cited Findings — DO 8 s.2015 (legacy, repealed by DO 15 s.2026) [KNOWN-PRIOR, from the official PDF: https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/DO_s2015_08.pdf]
- **Components:** Written Work (WW), Performance Tasks (PT), Quarterly Assessment (QA). Four quarters. Passing is 75. The lowest transmuted grade is 60. IG 60.00 → 75.
- **JHS (Grades 7–10) weights:**
  - Languages, AP, EsP: WW 30 / PT 50 / QA 20
  - Science, Math: 40 / 40 / 20
  - MAPEH, TLE: 20 / 60 / 20
- **SHS weights:**
  - Core subjects: 25 / 50 / 25
  - Academic track, all other subjects: 25 / 45 / 30
  - Academic track, Work Immersion / Research / Business Simulation / Exhibit / Performance: 35 / 40 / 25
  - TVL / Sports / Arts & Design, all other subjects: 20 / 60 / 20
  - TVL / Sports / Arts & Design, Work Immersion / Research / Exhibit / Performance: 20 / 60 / 20
- **Legacy transmutation table:**
  - IG 100 → 100; 98.40–99.99 → 99; … 60.00–61.59 → 75. Above 60, each step is 1.6 IG points.
  - Below 60: 56.00–59.99 → 74; … 0–3.99 → 60. Each step is 4 IG points.
- **Quarterly grade:** transmuted grade.
- **Final grade per learning area:** average of the 4 quarterly grades, rounded.
- **MAPEH quarterly grade:** average of the 4 components' quarterly grades.
- **General average:** average of final grades (whole number).
- **SHS:** quarterly grades per semester. The semester final grade = average of 2 quarters. The SHS GA is per semester.
- **Descriptors:** Outstanding 90–100, Very Satisfactory 85–89, Satisfactory 80–84, Fairly Satisfactory 75–79, Did Not Meet Expectations below 75.

### Inferences
- **Engine design:** a versioned "grading scheme" entity with:
  - periods (4 quarters / 2 semesters × 2 quarters / 3 terms);
  - components, with optional sub-components (EX = ST1 30 / ST2 30 / TE 40; VE domains);
  - a weight matrix keyed by (scheme, grade level / key stage, subject grading category);
  - a transmutation table, nullable, stored as IG ranges → TG;
  - a floor (60);
  - a passing mark (75);
  - rounding points (Term Grade, FG and GA rounded half-up to an integer; the IG is kept unrounded, at least 2 dp, for the table lookup);
  - a descriptor band set.
- A component with no weight (e.g., Research Electives EX) must be excluded, not treated as 0.
- Treat the "Grade 12 legacy + adjusted table" case as a hybrid scheme: DO 8 weights + DO 15 SY 2026–27 table + DO 15 terms/descriptors. Verify which descriptor set and period structure apply to that case.
- Half-up vs banker's rounding is not stated in the sources. DepEd practice is conventional half-up (.5 rounds up). Make it configurable.

### Gaps
- I did not find the numeric caps on WW/PT/EX per term.
- I did not find the official DO 15 PDF text. The SHS weights and the adjusted table come from one secondary source (DepEdClub), so cross-check them.
- The DO 8 s.2015 tables were reproduced from prior knowledge, not re-fetched. Verify against the PDF before seeding.
- It is unclear whether DO 15 term grading for SHS runs 3 terms/year with subjects completing within a term (semester-like). This depends on the DO 017 timetable.

## 4. Promotion, retention, remediation, attendance, records

### Takeaway
Under DO 15 s.2026 for Grades 4–12:
- pass all learning areas → promoted;
- fail at most 2 → Summer Remedial Class (SRC);
- fail more than 2 → retained.

SRC: the Recomputed Final Grade = average of the original FG and the Remedial Class Mark. RFG ≥ 75 → passed. Absences above 20% of class days → failing / no credit unless justified. Conditional promotion with back subjects is mentioned but the wording conflicts between sources.

### Cited Findings
- **Promotion rules (KS2–KS4):** pass all → promoted; fail ≤ 2 learning areas → SRC; fail > 2 → retained. Learners below standard receive documented remediation within instruction — [DepEdClub](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/)
- **SRC computation:** remedial assessments are computed like Term Grades → Remedial Class Mark (RCM). RFG = (FG + RCM)/2. RFG ≥ 75 = pass and promotion — [DepEdClub](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/)
- **After remediation:** learners still failing one or two areas "may be conditionally promoted and take back subjects, subject to policy provisions" — [tchersden](https://www.tchersden.com/2026/06/deped-order-015-2026-assessment-grading-awards-guide.html). This overlaps ambiguously with the SRC row and is **unverified**.
- **KS1 (K–3):** non-punitive promotion. Learners are not retained solely for age, a single assessment or academic performance — [DepEdClub](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/)
- **Attendance:** absences > 20% of prescribed class days → failing grade / no credit unless justified. The school head may grant documented case-by-case exemptions. Attendance is reported each term in the Learner Progress and Performance Report — [DepEdClub](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/)
- DepEd states that removing transmutation "should not be treated as an automatic trigger for higher retention." Promotion remains governed by existing promotion/retention policies — [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/06/22/2537040/deped-starts-phaseout-grade-transmutation-policy)
- **Context:** EDCOM 2's January 2026 report called transmutation part of "de facto mass promotion" and recommended its phase-out — [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/06/22/2537040/deped-starts-phaseout-grade-transmutation-policy)
- **Legacy DO 8 s.2015 [KNOWN-PRIOR]** ([PDF](https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/DO_s2015_08.pdf)):
  - JHS: fail ≤ 2 learning areas → remedial classes; fail ≥ 3 → retained.
  - Remedial Class Mark: Recomputed FG = (FG + RCM)/2; ≥ 75 passes.
  - Failing a remedial class allows promotion with back subjects, but one cannot carry more than 2 failed subjects.
  - SHS: a failed prerequisite subject must be passed before taking the dependent subject; a failed subject is taken via remediation/retake.
  - The Grade 10 / Grade 12 certificate of completion / diploma requires passing all learning areas.

### Inferences
- Store per learner per subject: `final_grade`, `rcm` (nullable), `recomputed_final_grade`, `remedial_status`, and `back_subject` flags.
- Promotion status is derived: count of failed FGs (or RFGs) → Promoted / For SRC / Retained / Conditionally Promoted.
- Absence percentage per subject or per learner must be computed against prescribed class days (configurable; DO 009 s.2026 sets the calendar). The 20% threshold is a default, with an override for an exemption record.
- "Incomplete" grades: no DO 15 rule was found. Model them as a nullable grade with a status of INC/pending, blocking promotion until resolved.

### Gaps
- I found no DO 15 provisions on incomplete grades, transferees (grade conversion between schemes or schools), or learners with disabilities (accommodations).
- The forms naming (SF9 vs the new "Learner Progress and Performance Report"; SF10 Learner Permanent Record) is not confirmed in DO 15 text. LRN-based permanent records continue under DepEd School Forms (not researched here).

## 5. Observed values / core values; attendance on SF9

### Takeaway
DO 8 s.2015 reported the 4 DepEd core values with a non-numerical scale on the SF9 report card:
- Core values: Maka-Diyos, Makatao, Makakalikasan, Makabansa.
- Scale: AO / SO / RO / NO.
- Attendance: days of school vs days present per month.

DO 15 s.2026 instead folds values into graded GMRC/Values Education (cognitive/affective/behavioral domains). I found **no evidence** about whether the separate observed-values section survives on the new report card.

### Cited Findings
- **DO 8 s.2015 [KNOWN-PRIOR]** ([PDF](https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/DO_s2015_08.pdf)):
  - Core values: Maka-Diyos, Makatao, Makakalikasan, Makabansa, each with behavior statements.
  - Marking: AO = Always Observed, SO = Sometimes Observed, RO = Rarely Observed, NO = Not Observed.
  - Reported quarterly. Non-numerical, not part of the GA.
  - The report card (SF9) shows attendance per month (school days, days present, days absent).
- **DO 15 s.2026:**
  - GMRC/VE grades include an Affective and a Behavioral domain (PT Behavioral 30%).
  - Attendance appears in the Learner Progress and Performance Report each term.
  - Sources: [DepEdClub](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/). [tchersden](https://www.tchersden.com/2026/06/deped-order-015-2026-assessment-grading-awards-guide.html) confirms its summary does not cover observed values.
- **K–3:** "Character Traits" non-academic recognition replaces academic honors — [SchoolFinderPH](https://www.schoolfinderph.com/blog/deped-new-grading-system-2026)

### Inferences
- Keep the observed-values module configurable: a value set and behavior statements as master data, a scale set (AO/SO/RO/NO default), and frequency per period. Default it ON for legacy schemes. Mark it "pending verification" for DO 15 schemes.
- Track attendance daily and roll it up per month (legacy SF9) and per term (DO 15).

### Gaps
- DO 15 s.2026's treatment of observed values and the new report card template were not found. This is a high priority to verify with the official PDF or annexes.

## 6. Recognition and awards (DO 36 s.2016 → DO 15 s.2026)

### Takeaway
DO 15 s.2026 **abolishes With Honors / With High Honors / With Highest Honors**. It replaces them with a single **Academic Excellence Award** for Grades 4–12, with three requirements:
- GA ≥ 90;
- no FG < 80;
- no derogatory or disciplinary record.

Awardees are listed **alphabetically, not ranked**. Other award categories are named but their criteria were not found. DO 36 s.2016 thresholds are legacy and needed for historical records.

### Cited Findings
- **Academic Excellence Award** (KS2–KS4):
  - GA ≥ 90; no FG < 80 in any learning area; no derogatory records or disciplinary cases within the SY.
  - Awardees are listed alphabetically. A school may certify actual rank on formal written request.
  - Sources: [SchoolFinderPH](https://www.schoolfinderph.com/blog/deped-new-grading-system-2026); [tchersden](https://www.tchersden.com/2026/06/deped-order-015-2026-assessment-grading-awards-guide.html); [DepEdClub](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/)
- **Honors tiers removed.** Circulating claims that the tiers survived, or of a 95-GA draft threshold, are inaccurate — [SchoolFinderPH](https://www.schoolfinderph.com/blog/deped-new-grading-system-2026)
- **Other categories named:** Excellence in Specific Learning Areas, Leadership Excellence, Special Recognition. No criteria in the source — [tchersden](https://www.tchersden.com/2026/06/deped-order-015-2026-assessment-grading-awards-guide.html)
- **KS1:** no academic awards. Non-academic recognition is allowed — [DepEdClub](https://depedclub.com/deped-grading-system-guidelines-do-015-s-2026/)
- **Legacy DO 36 s.2016 [KNOWN-PRIOR]** ([PDF](https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/DO_s2016_036.pdf)):
  - Academic Excellence Award (quarterly and year-end): With Honors GA 90–94, With High Honors 95–97, With Highest Honors 98–100. No final grade below 85 (year-end) or quarterly grade below 85 (quarterly). For SHS it is per semester.
  - Other awards: Leadership, Award for Outstanding Performance in specific disciplines, Conduct Award (based on core values ratings: AO in at least 75% of behavior statements), Perfect Attendance, Grade 12 Awards (Work Immersion, Research/Capstone).
  - **Inconsistency:** the original DO 36 did not settle whether the GA is rounded. DepEd later practice used the non-rounded GA for honors. Flag as **unverified**.

### Inferences
- The awards engine should be rule-based: eligibility predicates (GA threshold, min FG, no-disciplinary flag, attendance %), output ordering (alphabetical default), and a rank-certification report generated on request.
- Keep DO 36 tier rules as a legacy rule set for SY ≤ 2025–26 records.

### Gaps
- I did not find the criteria for DO 15 Excellence in Specific Learning Areas, Leadership Excellence or Special Recognition, or whether conduct and perfect-attendance awards remain.
- It is unclear whether the DO 15 Academic Excellence uses the rounded or unrounded GA, and whether it is year-end only or also per term.

## 7. National assessments (NAT G10/G12), ALS A&E

### Takeaway
NAT is administered to exiting Grade 10 and Grade 12 learners (and Grade 6). Recent results show near-zero proficiency, which drove the transmutation phase-out. ALS A&E certifies elementary and secondary completion for out-of-school learners. For a school management system, these are external result records linked to LRN rather than inputs to the grading engine.

### Cited Findings
- EDCOM 2, citing NAT 2023–2025, reports proficiency of 19.56% (Grade 6), 0.74% (Grade 10) and 0.40% (Grade 12) — search summary of a Rappler report (not fetched; **secondary**); context in [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/06/22/2537040/deped-starts-phaseout-grade-transmutation-policy)
- The GAA 2026 DepEd performance indicators list NAT proficiency targets for SY 2025–26: Grade 10 at 50% (no baseline) and Grade 12 at 28% vs 25.88% in SY 2023–24 — [DBM GAA 2026 Vol II DepEd](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/GAA/GAA2026/VolumeII/DEPED/A.pdf) (metric definition differs from EDCOM's figure, so the numbers are not comparable)
- **OECD review:** "Developing a national student assessment framework for the Philippines" (structure of NAT and other national assessments) — [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/developing-a-national-student-assessment-framework-for-the-philippines_786bff30-en/full-report/component-5.html)
- **ALS A&E 2025–26:**
  - Test held 29 March 2026 (rescheduled from 1 March). Results around 3 June 2026.
  - Passing score reportedly lowered to 60% from 75%.
  - Passers list via DM 037 s.2026 (naming inconsistent).
  - Source: [The Summit Express (secondary)](https://www.thesummitexpress.com/2026/05/als-ae-exam-results-2026-secondary.html)

### Inferences
- Model an `external_assessment` table (type NAT/A&E/ELLNA/other, grade level, SY, LRN, scores/proficiency level, passed flag). ALS A&E secondary passers are eligible for SHS enrollment (prior knowledge; verify) and need an entry pathway in enrollment.
- NAT results are not part of the GA or promotion.

### Gaps

- The official confirmation of the A&E passing-score change and its issuance number was not found.
